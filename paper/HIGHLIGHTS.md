# Highlights

Three to five bullets, each **no more than 85 characters including spaces**, free of
jargon, acronyms and abbreviations. Only some venues ask for this file; 85 characters is the
strictest limit among the candidates, so bullets that fit it fit any of them. See
`paper/journal_submission_notes.md` for which venues use it.

Character counts are verified by `scripts/check_highlights.py`, which fails if any bullet
exceeds the limit. The constraint is easy to break during editing, and submission systems
that use this file reject it rather than truncating it.

- First open station-level air quality benchmark for Central Asia
- Splits are frozen and checksummed before the reported results are produced
- Gradient boosting leads every admissible baseline at unmonitored sites
- Lowest error yet negative skill in three of six held-out cities
- Measured latency invalidated three of five availability assumptions
